About 關於

I study the infrastructure behind research evaluation: the bibliographic databases, identifiers, counting rules and rankings that decide which research is seen and how it is scored. Most of my work audits open infrastructure such as OpenAlex and the CWTS Leiden Ranking Open Edition at full-population scale, and asks how construction choices that look technical end up shaping rankings, hiring thresholds and policy.

I release the code and data behind each paper so that every result can be recomputed. Archived versions with citable DOIs are listed under Code & data.
