About 關於我

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Management of Technology, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taiwan. Most of my work audits open infrastructure such as OpenAlex and the CWTS Leiden Ranking Open Edition at full-population scale, and asks how construction choices that look technical end up shaping rankings, hiring thresholds and policy.

Before research I worked as a senior engineer in mold development and CNC machining. I release the code and data behind each paper so that every result can be recomputed.

國立陽明交通大學科技管理研究所博士生。我的研究多以完整母體規模稽核開放基礎設施，例如 OpenAlex 與萊頓排名開放版，追問那些看似技術性的建構選擇，如何影響排名、聘任門檻與政策。進入學術界前，我在綠點高新科技（Jabil）擔任模具開發與 CNC 加工資深工程師。每篇論文都公開程式與資料，讓每個結果都能重算。
