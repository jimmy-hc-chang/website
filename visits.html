València, Spain · from January 2027西班牙瓦倫西亞・2027 年 1 月起

Host 接待機構INGENIO (CSIC-UPV), Universitat Politècnica de València
Hosts 接待教授Jordi Molas-Gallart, José David Barberá-Tomás, Ismael Ràfols
Topic 研究主題Responsible innovation and transition governance in AI-enabled healthcare innovation ecosystems: Taiwan and Spain compared
人工智慧醫療創新生態系之負責任創新與轉型治理：臺灣與西班牙比較
Status 狀態Planned research stay 規劃中的移地研究

The stay continues the Taiwan–Spain project that NYCU and INGENIO carry out together (see Sydney 2026 below). The plan is to extend the comparison of AI-enabled healthcare innovation systems from Taiwan to Spain, and to complete a paper co-authored with Ismael Ràfols on whether AI-enabled research widens the gap between health research and global health needs.

延續陽明交大與 INGENIO 共同執行的臺灣—西班牙國際合作計畫（見下方 2026 雪梨一節），把 AI 醫療創新系統的比較從臺灣延伸到西班牙，並完成與 Ismael Ràfols 合著、探討 AI 是否拉大健康研究與全球健康需求落差的論文。

Sydney, Australia · March–September 2026澳洲雪梨・2026 年 3 月至 9 月

Host 接待機構Australian Artificial Intelligence Institute (AAII), School of Computer Science, University of Technology Sydney
Supervisor 指導教授Associate Professor Yi Zhang 張嶷
Topic 研究主題Responsible innovation ecosystems of artificial intelligence
人工智慧負責任創新生態系
Funding 經費NYCU Higher Education Sprout Project — doctoral student overseas research grant (115DR003)
陽明交大高教深耕計畫選送博士生赴國外移地研究補助（案號 115DR003）

I joined Yi Zhang's team at UTS, whose training is in algorithms and data engineering, to learn intelligent bibliometrics: processing papers and patents from many sources, identifying research topics and how they change, and combining this with network analysis of collaboration. I presented my research design at team meetings and received concrete advice on data cleaning, topic identification and presentation.

Working alongside the UTS engineers, I built TACIT, a Python tool for LLM-assisted qualitative coding that keeps the codebook's origin, the model's draft and the researcher's decision. It is installable with pip install tacit-qda, has been used on more than 50 interviews in our team, and its paper is under review at SoftwareX.

During the stay I presented 13 conference papers at seven international meetings, against a target of five, and travelled to Spain to carry out fieldwork for the NSTC–CSIC Taiwan–Spain project.

我加入雪梨科技大學張嶷副教授的研究團隊。團隊以演算法與資料工程為主，我在這裡學習智慧文獻計量：處理多來源的論文與專利資料、辨識研究主題及其演變，並結合社會網絡分析看機構間的合作結構。我在團隊會議報告研究設計，針對資料清理、主題辨識與結果呈現得到具體建議。

與 UTS 工程背景的成員共事，我開發了質性編碼軟體 TACIT：以大型語言模型輔助編碼，同時保留編碼簿來源、模型初稿與研究者的判斷。可用 pip install tacit-qda 安裝，已用於研究團隊 50 場以上訪談，論文已投 SoftwareX 並送外審。

移地研究期間在七場國際會議發表 13 篇論文（原訂 5 篇），並赴西班牙執行國科會與西班牙高等科學研究委員會（CSIC）的國際合作計畫。

Edinburgh, United Kingdom · June–September 2024英國愛丁堡・2024 年 6 月至 9 月

Host 接待機構Business School, Edinburgh Napier University
Supervisor 指導教授Luca Mora
Topic 研究主題Innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystems of smart technology industries and their sustainable transition
智慧科技產業的創新與創業生態系及其永續轉型
Funding 經費NYCU Higher Education Sprout Project — doctoral student overseas research grant
陽明交大高教深耕計畫選送博士生赴國外移地研究補助

Using national and sectoral innovation systems as the framework, I combined scientometric mapping, social network analysis, expert questionnaires and elite interviews to trace how Edinburgh's smart technology ecosystem formed and how its actors pursue sustainable transition. Napier provided an office, and Fabio Neves Da Rocha advised me on the research plan; I also took an online workshop on social network analysis methods.

From Edinburgh I presented at six international conferences, in person and online.

以國家／產業創新系統為架構，結合科學計量、社會網絡分析、專家問卷與菁英訪談，探討愛丁堡智慧科技產業生態系如何形成，以及其中的行動者如何實踐永續轉型。納皮爾大學提供研究室，Fabio Neves Da Rocha 教授就研究計畫給予指導；期間並參加社會網絡分析方法的線上工作坊。

以愛丁堡為基地，參加六場國際研討會（實體與線上）。

Thiruvananthapuram and Hyderabad, India · October 2023印度特里凡德朗與海德拉巴・2023 年 10 月

Partner 合作機構National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPER), Hyderabad
Project 計畫建構創新生態系：印度與台灣製藥及醫療器材產業之啟示（NSTC 111-2923-H-A49-001-MY2, international cooperation project）
Building innovation ecosystems: lessons from the pharmaceutical and medical device industries of India and Taiwan [English rendering]
Dates 期間GLOBELICS 2023, 12–14 Oct; fieldwork in Hyderabad, 15–20 Oct 2023
GLOBELICS 2023：10 月 12–14 日；海德拉巴田野研究：10 月 15–20 日

At the GLOBELICS 2023 conference, held at the Gulati Institute of Finance and Taxation in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, I presented a paper on the industrial transformation of the pharmaceutical innovation systems of Taiwan and India, and co-presented a comparative paper with our Indian partner NIPER Hyderabad.

The research team then spent five days in Hyderabad, India's pharmaceutical hub, interviewing institutes, incubators and firms across the city's innovation ecosystem. I also presented our project to NIPER students.

於喀拉拉邦特里凡德朗古拉蒂財政與稅收研究所舉行的 GLOBELICS 2023 國際研討會，發表臺灣與印度製藥創新系統產業轉型的論文，並與印度合作對象海德拉巴藥物研究所（NIPER）共同發表兩國製藥創新生態系的比較研究。

會後研究團隊在印度製藥中心海德拉巴進行五天田野研究，訪談當地創新生態系中的研究機構、育成中心與企業；我也向 NIPER 學生介紹本團隊的研究計畫。
